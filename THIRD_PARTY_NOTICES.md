# Third-party sources and attribution

The repository's MIT license applies to original project code and authored prose. References to publications, source metadata and numerical facts do not grant rights to the publishers' underlying works, marks or datasets. No claim is made that all referenced material is openly licensed.

Source records identify the original publisher, URL, verification date and limitations. Consult those records and each publisher's terms before downloading, adapting or redistributing source material. Full third-party reports, article bodies, publisher logos and bulk government/media registers are not bundled.

The small numerical sample in `statistical-observations.json` is attributed to Nepal Rastra Bank's Bhadra 2083 Payment Systems Indicators. Original units, periods and caveats are retained; counts must not be interpreted as unique people. Administrative and media-register research counts in coverage notes describe a prior research snapshot, not data supplied by this package.

Other principal discovery publishers include Nepal's National Statistics Office, government departments and regulators, public educational institutions, research producers, Press Council Nepal and individual news publishers. The full attribution list is the bundled `source-registry.json`.

This public skill does not incorporate the separately installed marketing-skills collection or Agent Reach implementation. References to analytical methods are original workflow guidance, not reproduced textbooks or a claim of endorsement by a cited institution.
