# Nepal technology-business intelligence: operating method

## Scope of the tree

The root is Nepal's technology-business sector: the technology supply, institutions and customers, people and knowledge, laws and finance, and the external conditions that materially affect demand, cost, capability, risk or delivery. Nepal is the market and geographical boundary. International engineering documentation or an external event is relevant only to a specific Nepal dependency or decision; do not expand this into general world news.

Use the 60 domains and 600 research leaves as an expandable taxonomy. Each leaf needs evidence, definitions, dates, geography, relationships, indicators and decisions. The extra engineering branches are finer research prompts and may overlap the core topics. Their presence does not mean they were researched. Expand a demonstrated missing branch; do not claim a final taxonomy that can never miss a new technology or local condition.

## Work a leaf deeply

For the leaf selected by the task:

1. Define the question, technology, buyer/institution, relevant locality and decision. Link the appropriate domain and locality codes.
2. Establish the baseline: owning-source data, exact reference period, unit, denominator, effective rules, existing capability and infrastructure. If it is unavailable, record the missing measurement.
3. Examine actors and incentives: users, technical evaluators, approvers, budget holders, suppliers, installers, support teams, regulators, education/research bodies and affected citizens. Use verified institutional roles, not inferred private relationships.
4. Trace causes and constraints: policy, released funding, procurement process, power, connectivity, terrain, transport, season, language, skills, currency, imports, credit, maintenance and lawful data use.
5. Distinguish an observed need, an idea, an announcement, a budget proposal, approved allocation, released funds, procurement, evaluation, award, deployment, acceptance and measured outcome. Attach evidence to each stage independently.
6. Apply relevant methods from `principles-and-methods.json`: comparable denominators, sampling, bottom-up markets, unit economics, cash flow, lifecycle cost, management control and uncertainty. Name assumptions. Do not insert unsupported numbers to fill a table.
7. Produce a Nepal-specific decision with source links, uncertainty, practical next action, responsible institution, evidence still needed and verification date. Preserve field feedback and contradictory evidence.

These are decision steps, not a requirement to write seven sections for a narrow query. An exhaustive assignment should retain the detailed evidence ledger and coverage gaps rather than compressing everything to a headline.

## Relations are as important as topics

Represent links such as `locality → institution → problem → budget/programme → procurement → offered configuration → installation → acceptance → support`; and `policy/currency/hazard/skills → business mechanism → affected technology/buyers → observable indicator`. A registration, media story or national statistic must not jump directly to a confirmed buyer or sale.

Use stable IDs for domains, sources, localities and observations. Keep entity aliases and raw names. The local code is unique only with its district code in this directory. Never join every local code `1` across Nepal as one municipality. Maintain source, boundary version and calendar distinctions.

## Geographic coverage

The acquired NSO directory covers 7 provinces, 77 districts and 753 local levels using NPHC2021 baseline dropdown data. It does not supply a currently verified official website for each local government. Census ward counts are dated baseline values. The official map geometry was not used as the authority for the directory because it contained duplicate or inconsistent features.

For each place, independently seek its current official website, ward/institution directory, annual plans and budget, procurement notices, actual service assets, schools/health providers/enterprises, infrastructure constraints, local suppliers and technology reports. Maintain a local coverage ledger to retain gaps; the bulk directory is not bundled in the public edition. A national source, a provincial sample or an outlet headquartered in a province must not be marked as municipal evidence.

Include rural municipalities, urban municipalities, sub-metropolitan and metropolitan areas as represented in the owning source. Do not make Kathmandu or already familiar Lumbini places stand in for Nepal. A ward-level claim needs explicit ward-level evidence; do not infer it from a municipality name.

## Statistics and business principles

Keep measured observations separate from methods and hypotheses. Every observation should retain the original value and unit, numerator/denominator where relevant, stock or flow basis, geography, reference period, publication date, source table/page, edition and caveats. A census year, fiscal year, BS calendar date, upload-path year and retrieval date are different fields.

Wallet registrations are not unique people. Installed equipment is not active use. A programme target is not an outcome. A province estimate is not a municipal estimate. A changed exchange rate is an input to a landed-cost calculation, not a completed price quotation.

The educational and analytical methods supply useful decision principles. The presence of a subject in a TU curriculum does not prove every named framework is taught in that edition or that its application will succeed in Nepal. Validate frameworks against the client's actual conditions and field evidence.

## Media discovery to technology intelligence

The official online-media, newspaper and FM registers are the discovery base. Their bulk extracts are not bundled in the public edition; obtain them from the original publishers subject to applicable terms. They are historical and can contain duplicates, typos, missing serials, obsolete domains and font-extraction artifacts. They are not an active-outlet list or proof of reliability. Keep the source row/page and raw string. The selected publisher checks and seven province-category routes are separate evidence.

Progress each source through distinct states: registry acquired → row extracted → domain verified → feed observed → feed tested → article retrieved → Nepal relevance established → technology relevance classified → locality validated → claim corroborated → continuously monitored. Do not skip states when reporting coverage. No continuously running media monitor was deployed by this knowledge-building task.

Keep only:

- **Direct technology signals:** substantive ICT demand, capability, research, jobs, infrastructure, supply, failure, deployment, cybersecurity, digital services or procurement with an explicit Nepal nexus.
- **External business signals:** a documented Nepal technology-sector impact through tax, currency, imports, credit, policy, budgets, labour, power, hazards or transport. Record the causal mechanism and affected segment.
- **Review queue:** ambiguous location, incidental technology wording, unclear dates, unsupported monetary/approval claims or uncertain external mechanisms.

Exclude unrelated general politics, crime, entertainment, civil/electrical work and foreign gadget releases without a demonstrated Nepal technology connection. A local infrastructure project qualifies only where substantive ICT scope or an explicit ICT dependency is evidenced.

For each retained story, capture publisher, URL, title, publication date/calendar, event date, geography, institution, domain, signal type, reported claim, short evidence reference, official corroboration, contradiction, next action, retrieval time and origin/duplicate cluster. Cite the original publisher; syndicated copies do not become independent corroboration. News reports are leads unless their claims have the required independent owning-source support. Do not copy entire articles into shareable knowledge packages.

## Source reliability and freshness

Prefer the responsible agency, regulator, statistics producer, legal instrument, official tender, exact OEM documentation or direct research producer for the claim being made. Use national/provincial/local media for reported events and discovery; verify authoritative facts with the actual owner. Source registration, successful HTTP, a search excerpt, an opened page and an audited dataset are separate levels of evidence.

Dates in the source registry are checks performed on 2026-11-11, not perpetual validity. Recheck volatile rules, deadlines, prices, stocks, positions, source availability and legal status when used. Historical source material remains useful only with its original period. Draft consultation, proposal, enacted rule and implementation record must remain distinguishable.

## Coverage and expansion

Use `coverage.json` for current snapshot counts. Use locality and media frontiers to find the next unverified source. Do not multiply a taxonomy by 753 places and describe the combinations as researched facts. Complete coverage would require ongoing source discovery, access verification, article and document review, local validation, correction and updates; it is not established by this release.

Research tools must not alter production, contact institutions, send messages, publish, spend, submit bids or commit prices without the appropriate authorization. Prepare reviewable intelligence and artifacts within the current task. Future monitoring needs a scoped implementation and evidence of operation before it is described as live.
