# D08: Company registration and governance

Branch: Business foundations. Market and geographic scope: Nepal.

## Decision principle

The legal entity, trading identity and signing authority must match the transaction.

This is analytical guidance. It is not a measured Nepal outcome or a legal/engineering certification.

Use only metrics that fit the actual question: filing status, authority records, unresolved obligations. Specify units, denominators and periods; no values are supplied by this taxonomy.

These are candidate discovery routes. Read their verification and limitations in `source-registry.json`; an accessible homepage does not prove full content coverage.

## Nepal field check

Resolve the locality using the official-code directory. Examine the local authority, actual institution, relevant budget/notice or market record, nearby support capacity, language, power/connectivity, transport/hazard constraints, and the current status of the proposed action. A national figure must not be assigned to each municipality.
