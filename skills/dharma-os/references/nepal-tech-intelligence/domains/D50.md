# D50: Digital government identity and public service platforms

Branch: Sector demand. Market and geographic scope: Nepal.

## Decision principle

Digitization should reduce a measured service problem and preserve lawful authority and citizen access.

This is analytical guidance. It is not a measured Nepal outcome or a legal/engineering certification.

## Measurements

Use only metrics that fit the actual question: completed service, turnaround time, service failure. Specify units, denominators and periods; no values are supplied by this taxonomy.

## Nepal field check

Resolve the locality using the official-code directory. Examine the local authority, actual institution, relevant budget/notice or market record, nearby support capacity, language, power/connectivity, transport/hazard constraints, and the current status of the proposed action. A national figure must not be assigned to each municipality.
