# D17: Content media and demand generation

Branch: Markets and customers. Market and geographic scope: Nepal.

## Decision principle

Judge content by useful buyer action and evidence, not posting volume alone.

This is analytical guidance. It is not a measured Nepal outcome or a legal/engineering certification.

## Nepal field check

Resolve the locality using the official-code directory. Examine the local authority, actual institution, relevant budget/notice or market record, nearby support capacity, language, power/connectivity, transport/hazard constraints, and the current status of the proposed action. A national figure must not be assigned to each municipality.
