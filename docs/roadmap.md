# Help improve one leaf

## Available in 0.2

The offline browser workspace, evidence-aware study format, six research protocols, four worked desk studies, selected 50-observation payment dataset, citation/export tools and structural checks are implemented. The domain coverage ledger records where selected studies exist. See [the upgrade review](upgrade-review.md) for the tested behavior and remaining limits.

## Research priorities

| Priority | Work | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One institution-level school readiness study | Verified identity, permission, observed teaching/site conditions, funding stage, support plan and local reviewer |
| 2 | One real ICT tender evidence review | Official document/amendments, clause-to-evidence matrix, exact-model references and authorized review |
| 3 | Provincial access/skills/business evidence | Current owning sources, original definitions/periods, reproducible extraction and geographic limits |
| 4 | A bounded municipal research pilot | Defined locality set, code/boundary provenance, dated official sources, missingness and field checks |
| 5 | Active technology-media sample | Retrieved articles, dates, locality verification, syndication clusters and corroboration |
| 6 | Research usability study | Consenting student/business participants, task completion observations, reproducibility failures and corrections |

These are open research tasks, not promises of already completed data collection. None requires a national completion claim. Do not extrapolate a pilot to all municipalities or describe discovered outlets as monitored sources.

## Contribution paths

Contributions are welcome at a small, verifiable scale:

1. **Correct one source:** verify the owning publisher, URL, source date, geographic scope and limits.
2. **Add one local case:** supply public evidence for a Nepal institution or locality and identify the exact technology connection. Keep reported plans separate from completed outcomes.
3. **Improve one method:** explain the decision it supports, the evidence required and the failure it prevents.
4. **Check one measurement:** preserve the unit, denominator, reference period and original table/page.
5. **Improve one workflow:** demonstrate an actual user outcome with a reproducible test.

Larger open research areas include current provincial/local source directories, dated sector statistics, engineering evidence, active publisher verification and careful locality mapping. Bulk-source redistribution requires a separate rights review. Continuous media monitoring is not implemented.

Use the source-correction or research-gap issue form. Contributions must be public, attributable and free of private contact lists, credentials and client/employee records.
