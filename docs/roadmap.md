# Help improve one leaf

Contributions are welcome at a small, verifiable scale:

1. **Correct one source:** verify the owning publisher, URL, source date, geographic scope and limits.
2. **Add one local case:** supply public evidence for a Nepal institution or locality and identify the exact technology connection. Keep reported plans separate from completed outcomes.
3. **Improve one method:** explain the decision it supports, the evidence required and the failure it prevents.
4. **Check one measurement:** preserve the unit, denominator, reference period and original table/page.
5. **Improve one workflow:** demonstrate an actual user outcome with a reproducible test.

Larger open research areas include current provincial/local source directories, dated sector statistics, engineering evidence, active publisher verification and careful locality mapping. Bulk-source redistribution requires a separate rights review. Continuous media monitoring is not implemented.

Use the source-correction or research-gap issue form. Contributions must be public, attributable and free of private contact lists, credentials and client/employee records.
