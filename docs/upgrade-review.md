## Evaluation

An independent AI-agent forward test used only the portable skill and offline artifacts to answer a student question connecting QR growth with school technology demand. It reproduced descriptive national changes and correctly left local school need, readiness and benefit unestablished. The evaluation discovered the search/discovery gap and national-evidence relabelling defect; both were corrected and retested. A follow-up export issue was corrected and covered by a browser-core regression case.

This is a scoped behavioral test, not an independent academic peer review or a claim of real customer adoption. Python tests cover source traceability, unsupported facts, malformed inputs, dates, contradictions, chronological/unit comparisons, circular reasoning, no-overwrite behavior, exports and overlapping statistics. The browser core exercises the corresponding important evidence states and catalog consistency. UI checks cover local-file loading, search, creating a student plan, rejecting an unsupported claim and opening a worked study. Broader device and accessibility testing remains open.

## Research still required

Only selected payment evidence has expanded in this upgrade. Most domain leaves, current provincial/local source records, local budgets and actual institution needs, broader statistics, active technology reporting and engineering dossiers remain open. There is no continuous crawler or monitoring deployment. The three payment studies use one publication and are not independent corroboration of one another.

The next research contribution should complete a bounded question with owning-source evidence, reproducible extraction/analysis and an appropriate local or subject reviewer. More topic counts cannot substitute for that work.

The upgrade also attempted current official Banganga budget access and NSO household/province evidence retrieval. Direct access timed out or returned 502; those candidates remain in [the verification frontier](../sources/next-verifications.json). Search-visible values were not silently promoted to audited observations. A failed request is not proof that a source, budget or opportunity is absent.
