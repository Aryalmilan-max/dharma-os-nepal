# QR payments: volume growth is not customer growth

Question: How did reported monthly QR volume and nominal amount change between Bhadra 2082 and 2083?
Status: desk_review · Period: Source-specific periods stated with each claim
Geography: Nepal national

**Research artifact — human source and method review required.**

## Claims and evidence

### Q1 · fact

NRB reports 41,087,808 QR transactions in Bhadra 2082 and 75,581,982 in Bhadra 2083.

- supports: [Monthly Payment Systems Indicators: Bhadra 2083 (Mid-Sept 2026)](https://www.nrb.org.np/psd/payment-systems-indicators-of-2083-bhadra/); Page 1, B13, transaction columns for 2082 and 2083 Bhadra; period Mid Aug–Mid Sept 2025 and Mid Aug–Mid Sept 2026; geography Nepal national; checked 2026-11-12.

Limitations: National descriptive evidence only; no locality or causal inference.

### Q2 · fact

The corresponding nominal amounts are NPR 101,239 million and NPR 167,933 million.

- supports: [Monthly Payment Systems Indicators: Bhadra 2083 (Mid-Sept 2026)](https://www.nrb.org.np/psd/payment-systems-indicators-of-2083-bhadra/); Page 1, B13, amount columns for 2082 and 2083 Bhadra; period Mid Aug–Mid Sept 2025 and Mid Aug–Mid Sept 2026; geography Nepal national; checked 2026-11-12.

Limitations: National descriptive evidence only; no locality or causal inference.

### Q3 · inference

Volume increased 83.95% and nominal amount 65.88%. Formula: (later/earlier − 1) × 100. This is descriptive year-on-year change in the printed monthly totals.

Basis: Q1, Q2


Limitations: National descriptive evidence only; no locality or causal inference.
